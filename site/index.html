About the project

Not just a login page. A full ecosystem with rules.

CryptoStar connects a public educational website with a private app. The public website introduces the idea, risks and structure. The app is where users register, receive their platform ID, submit deposit or withdrawal requests, complete lessons, manage profile data and follow reward progress.

The platform does not promise guaranteed income. Important balance changes and requests are reviewed by an administrator.

Earning logic

A clear model for activity, rewards and withdrawals.

Users can receive internal rewards through lessons, satellite structure, trading streaks and balance milestones. The exact rules are summarized below so a new user can understand the model without reading a long block of text.

$100For an invited user after a confirmed deposit from $2,000+.
10%For each active satellite, calculated from the satellite's current balance.
View full reward and withdrawal rules

An invited user may receive one $100 reward after a confirmed deposit from $2,000+. An active satellite has a confirmed deposit from $250. The inviter can claim 10% of each active satellite's current balance with a separate button for each satellite.

Balance rewards use fixed milestones: $50 at $1,000, $100 at $2,500, $200 at $5,000, $500 at $10,000, $1,000 at $20,000, $2,500 at $50,000 and $5,000 at $100,000.

Withdrawal requests start from $100 and only use earned funds. A 7.5% fee is applied before administrator review.

Questions and answers

Typical questions before joining.

Is this website separate from the app?

Yes. This website is a public project presentation. The app is separate and handles login, registration, learning, assets, rewards, trading and admin-reviewed requests.

Does the project guarantee profit?

No. Crypto markets are volatile and involve risk. The platform provides structure, education and internal accounting, but it cannot remove market risk or guarantee income.

How do users receive access to learning?

Learning opens after a confirmed deposit from $1,000. The app shows available lessons, tests and reward status.

Is there a group for communication?

The project will have a WhatsApp group for questions, updates and communication. The invitation link will be added after the community is prepared.

Can withdrawals go to another wallet?

Yes. The withdrawal wallet can be different from the wallet attached to the account, as long as the address is valid and the request follows the platform rules.

What are satellites?

Satellites are users invited by another user. They become active after a confirmed deposit from $250. The inviter can claim 10% of each active satellite's current balance.

Why are deposit and withdrawal requests reviewed by an admin?

Admin review keeps balance changes controlled, traceable and connected to each user. It also creates a clear signal history inside the admin panel.
